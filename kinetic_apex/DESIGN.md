## Brand & Style

This design system channels the raw kinetic energy of elite athletics, the deliberate discipline of modern gym culture, and the refined edge of high-end streetwear. The aesthetic is engineered to deliver visceral impact: visceral velocity paired with structural precision.

The style merges **High-Contrast Boldness** with **Technical Minimalism**:
- **Attitude**: Confident, hyper-focused, uncompromising, and aspirational.
- **Visual Weight**: Massive compressed typography colliding with hyper-clean, utilitarian data points and generous breathing room.
- **Imagery & Atmosphere**: Studio-lit performance apparel, grit-infused gym textures, stark concrete architectural backdrops, and motion-blurred athleticism anchored by a razor-sharp, monochromatic frame punctuated with high-voltage orange.

## Colors

The palette establishes an aggressive visual hierarchy driven by stark contrast and pure energetic release.

### Core Roles
- **Primary Accent (`#FF5A1F`)**: "Electric Orange" — reserved exclusively for primary calls-to-action, active indicators, sale notifications, live workout telemetry, and high-impact focal points.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#FF4100`)**: "Ignition Red-Orange" — calibrated for hover transitions, active press states, and urgent stock notifications.
- **Base Canvas & Surfaces**:
  - `Surface Void` (`#0B0B0B`): Ultimate canvas background, creating deep blacks that isolate apparel photography.
  - `Surface Layer 1` (`#121212`): Primary card containers, modular tiles, and floating navbars.
  - `Surface Layer 2` (`#18181B`): Secondary surfaces, input fills, dropdown panels, and modal layers.
- **Neutrals & Borders**:
  - `Border Subtle` (`#27272A`): 1px structural container definition.
  - `Border Contrast` (`#3F3F46`): Interactive hover boundaries and focused field rings.
  - `Concrete Pale` (`#F4F4F5` / `#E4E4E7`): Inverted mode surfaces, sub-labels, and high-contrast chips.
- **Text & Foreground**:
  - `White Pure` (`#FFFFFF`): Primary editorial headlines, bold labels, and hero copy.
  - `Zinc Muted` (`#A1A1AA`): Supporting product descriptions, category meta tags, and inactive states.

## Typography

The typographic strategy orchestrates a deliberate clash between **explosive, condensed display power** and **surgical, neutral utility**.

- **Display & Headlines (`Anton`)**: Always uppercase. Set with tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) and near-solid line heights (`1.0`). Headline text commands the space like stadium signage, campaign billboards, and heavy iron plates.
- **Body & Metadata (`Inter`)**: Set in sentence case or precise uppercase tags. Prioritizes extreme legibility for technical fabric specs, sizing recommendations, and cart management.
- **Letter Spacing Directives**: All label tokens (`label-lg`, `label-md`, `label-sm`) require uppercase transforms with widened tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to balance the density of technical sport badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a rigorous **8px base grid** designed for both dense e-commerce grids and expansive hero showcases.

- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: 1200px to 1440px max content width. Fluid margins scaling up to `margin-lg` (80px), gutters fixed at `gutter-lg` (32px).
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Gutters at `gutter` (24px), edge margins at `margin-md` (48px).
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: Gutters at `gutter-sm` (16px), margins at `margin` (24px).
- **Rhythm**: Generous vertical section offsets (`5rem` to `7.5rem`) separate drop announcements, lookbooks, and category shelves, creating tension between hyper-dense technical spec clusters and open negative space.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids soft, decorative skeuomorphism in favor of **Tonal Layering** and **Crisp Structural Boundaries**.

- **Surface Elevation Hierarchy**:
  - `Level 0 (Floor)`: `#0B0B0B` (Canvas base).
  - `Level 1 (Modules)`: `#121212` with a `1px solid #27272A` perimeter.
  - `Level 2 (Overlays & Drawers)`: `#18181B` with a `1px solid #3F3F46` edge and an ambient drop shadow: `0px 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`.
  - `Level 3 (Toasts & Floating Action Strips)`: `#27272A` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Lighting Dynamics**: No colored glow artifacts except for the Electric Orange CTA focus states, which deploy an assertive `0px 0px 0px 3px rgba(255, 90, 31, 0.45)` ring.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a high-impact contrast in its geometry: **ultra-sharp framing paired with tactile, full-radius pills**.

- **Pill Philosophy (`rounded-full` / `3rem`)**: Primary callouts, filter chips, action pills, size pills, and search capsules are purely rounded to feel athletic, ergonomic, and aerodynamic.
- **Modular Shells (`rounded-sm` / `0.25rem` to `rounded-md` / `0.5rem`)**: Editorial product cards, lookbook tiles, media containers, and modal windows maintain sharp, architectural perimeters to prevent the interface from feeling playful or soft.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Athletic Pill**: Full pill radius. Background: `#FFFFFF`, text: `#0B0B0B`, typography: `label-lg` uppercase. Hover triggers a transition to `#FF5A1F` background with `#FFFFFF` text, scaling up by `1.02` with immediate tactile feedback.
- **Secondary Dark Pill**: Border: `1.5px solid #3F3F46`, background: `transparent`, text: `#FFFFFF`. Hover switches background to `#18181B` and border to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Accent Ignition Pill**: Background: `#FF5A1F`, text: `#FFFFFF`. Hover shifts to `#FF4100`.

### Product Cards
- **Container**: Surface `#121212`, 1px border `#27272A`. Image ratio 4:5 or 1:1 against a cold `#18181B` or concrete backdrop.
- **Top Bar Badging**: Left side houses status chips; right side holds a floating circular Wishlist Heart toggle (transparent zinc icon turning `#FF5A1F` filled on active).
- **Quick-Add Overlay**: Slides up from the card bottom on desktop hover (or permanently anchored on mobile bottom-sheet); displays rapid sizing pill selectors (`S`, `M`, `L`, `XL`) directly inside the card.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Tags**: Uppercase, tight padding (`space-xs` vertical, `space-sm` horizontal), `label-sm` font.
  - `NEW`: Pure white background, black text.
  - `SALE` / `LIMITED`: Electric Orange `#FF5A1F` background, white text.
  - `BESTSELLER`: Border `1px solid #3F3F46`, zinc muted text.
- **Filter Chips**: Pill shape, background `#18181B`, border `#27272A`. Selected state: `#FFFFFF` fill with `#0B0B0B` text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields**: Height 48px, background `#121212`, border `1px solid #27272A`, pill-shaped or rounded `0.375rem`. Text: `#FFFFFF`, placeholder: `#71717A`.
- **Focus**: Border `#FF5A1F`, outer ring `2px rgba(255, 90, 31, 0.2)`.

### Wishlist & Micro-interactions
- **Heart Toggle**: 40px circular surface `#18181B` with 50% opacity backdrop blur. On click, executes a rapid spring scale animation (`1.2` scale down to `1.0`) filling the icon with `#FF5A1F`.
- **Cart Flyout Drawer**: Persistent right-side anchored sheet in `#0B0B0B`, bordered on the left by `1px solid #27272A`, calculating total weights, shipping meters with animated `#FF5A1F` progress bars, and high-impact sticky pill checkouts.
